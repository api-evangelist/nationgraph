# NationGraph

> NationGraph makes the public sector legible and accessible to businesses. We index ~110,000 government institutions and ~4 million government webpages to surface predictive buying intelligence for sales teams selling to state, local, and education (SLED) government.

## Products

- [Signals](https://nationgraph.com/signals): Predictive intelligence on government purchasing decisions. Cross-references council meetings, budgets, purchase orders, contracts, capital improvement plans, and active RFPs to surface real buying triggers.
- [Contacts](https://nationgraph.com/contacts): AI-powered search that matches government decision-makers by role, with verified emails and direct phone numbers.
- [Public Record Automations](https://nationgraph.com/automations): Unlocks hard-to-access government documents at scale. Handles FOIA requests, extraction, and structured delivery into existing sales tools.
- [Automations](https://nationgraph.com/automations): Turn signals and public-sector data into repeatable workflows — ranked institution lists, competitor contract research via FOIA, and CRM syncs.
